Land Acknowledgment

We, the Sigma Upsilon chapter of Delta Sigma Pi, acknowledge that we are on the traditional land of the first people of Seattle - the Coast Salish peoples, which encompasses the Duwamish, Suquamish, Tulalip, and Muckleshoot nations - past and present with honor and gratitude for the land itself and the respective tribes.
